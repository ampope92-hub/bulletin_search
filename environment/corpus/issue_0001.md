Studio Bulletin, issue 1

## Item 1
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Raman microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 5
The plaster wall relief is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 6
Radiography of the marble portrait bust identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the marble portrait bust used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 9
The bronze votive figure is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 10
The plaster wall relief is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 11
The lacquer writing box is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 12
A failed earlier repair in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with hidden hardwood pegs. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.
