Studio Bulletin, issue 2

## Item 1
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 4
A failed earlier repair in the linen sampler was rejoined with thread-by-thread bridging. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 5
The porcelain dish was rehoused in an aluminium honeycomb panel, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 6
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the bronze votive figure following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the marble portrait bust with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 11
Raman microscopy of the vellum choir book identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.
