Studio Bulletin, issue 4

## Item 1
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the silver caddy spoon following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 2
The vellum choir book is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 3
Polarised light microscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
The silk altar frontal is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 5
The pewter tankard is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the papier-mache tea tray used a seaweed-derived size, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 9
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the vellum choir book with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 11
The silk altar frontal is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the wool tapestry with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
