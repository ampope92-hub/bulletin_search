Conservation Notes, issue 6

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the pewter tankard with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the linen sampler was treated by localised heat treatment. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 4
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the plaster wall relief following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the porcelain dish with a cellulose poultice, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed insect frass from the papier-mache tea tray with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 7
The bronze votive figure is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 8
The linen sampler is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 9
A through crack in the papier-mache tea tray was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 10
The pewter tankard is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 11
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the pewter tankard following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 12
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.
