Conservation Notes, issue 18

## Item 1
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 2
Infrared spectroscopy of the plaster wall relief identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the marble portrait bust with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the oak chest following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 6
Treatment of the flaking paint on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the papier-mache tea tray with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 11
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the papier-mache tea tray used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 12
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
