Studio Bulletin, issue 21

## Item 1
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a seaweed-derived size, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
A detached handle in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the plaster wall relief with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 5
A failed earlier repair in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 6
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the silver caddy spoon following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the plaster wall relief with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the wool tapestry with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed insect frass from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
The leather bookbinding was rehoused in a sunken window mat, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 12
X-ray fluorescence of the wool tapestry identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.
