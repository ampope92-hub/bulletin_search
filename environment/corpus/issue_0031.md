Conservation Notes, issue 31

## Item 1
Cleaning removed insect frass from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed insect frass from the ivory hair comb with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the wool tapestry with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the ivory hair comb with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 5
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the marble portrait bust identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 6
The marble portrait bust was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 8
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the silver caddy spoon following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 9
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the pewter tankard following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 10
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the linen sampler was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a wheat starch paste, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 12
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.
