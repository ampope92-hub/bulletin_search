Studio Bulletin, issue 34

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the glass roundel with a cellulose poultice, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the silk altar frontal identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 3
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the lacquer writing box was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the wool tapestry was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 8
X-ray fluorescence of the linen sampler identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 9
The leather bookbinding is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the papier-mache tea tray used a microcrystalline wax, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 11
The linen sampler is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.
