Collections Care Digest, issue 35

## Item 1
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the pewter tankard following corrosion inhibition with an azole. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
Radiography of the leather bookbinding identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 6
A detached handle in the oak chest was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 7
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the wool tapestry was treated by localised heat treatment. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 8
Infrared spectroscopy of the leather bookbinding identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 9
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
The silk altar frontal is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 11
The plaster wall relief is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
A through crack in the ivory hair comb was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.
