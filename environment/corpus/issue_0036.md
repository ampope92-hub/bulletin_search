Conservation Notes, issue 36

## Item 1
Radiography of the leather bookbinding identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 2
Infrared spectroscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 4
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 5
The leather bookbinding was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with washed cotton jersey. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the marble portrait bust with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 7
Radiography of the wool tapestry identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silver caddy spoon with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 10
The marble portrait bust is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a seaweed-derived size, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.
