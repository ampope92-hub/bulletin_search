Conservation Notes, issue 48

## Item 1
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used an isinglass solution, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the porcelain dish with a cellulose poultice, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the silk altar frontal was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 4
The leather bookbinding was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 5
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed insect frass from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 7
The pewter tankard was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 8
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with grated vinyl crumb, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
Treatment of the flaking paint on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 12
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.
