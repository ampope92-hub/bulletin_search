Collections Care Digest, issue 53

## Item 1
Infrared spectroscopy of the plaster wall relief identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 2
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 3
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed insect frass from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the bronze votive figure following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 6
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the oak chest used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 7
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the plaster wall relief with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the plaster wall relief used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the marble portrait bust with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 11
X-ray fluorescence of the bronze votive figure identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the ivory hair comb following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.
