Collections Care Digest, issue 55

## Item 1
The ivory hair comb was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with polyester wadding. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 2
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 3
The oak chest is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the glass roundel with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the plaster wall relief with a cellulose poultice, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 6
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the silk altar frontal was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 7
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the lacquer writing box following consolidation of the powdering surface. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used an isinglass solution, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 9
A detached handle in the pewter tankard was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 10
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the bronze votive figure following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.
