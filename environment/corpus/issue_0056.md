Collections Care Digest, issue 56

## Item 1
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the silk altar frontal identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
The pewter tankard was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 6
Raman microscopy of the ivory hair comb identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
Infrared spectroscopy of the marble portrait bust identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the ivory hair comb with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the papier-mache tea tray following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 11
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the papier-mache tea tray identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silk altar frontal with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.
