Studio Bulletin, issue 60

## Item 1
The vellum choir book is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 2
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 3
X-ray fluorescence of the silver caddy spoon identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used an isinglass solution, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 5
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 6
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the papier-mache tea tray used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the glass roundel identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 8
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the bronze votive figure following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 9
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the leather bookbinding identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silver caddy spoon with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
Radiography of the papier-mache tea tray identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.
