Conservation Notes, issue 65

## Item 1
The pewter tankard is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 2
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 3
Polarised light microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the glass roundel following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 5
The silk altar frontal is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a wheat starch paste, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
The oak chest is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
The glass roundel is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 10
The plaster wall relief is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
The marble portrait bust is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the pewter tankard with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.
