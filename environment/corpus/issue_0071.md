Collections Care Digest, issue 71

## Item 1
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 2
Raman microscopy of the papier-mache tea tray identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 3
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the papier-mache tea tray following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 4
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the plaster wall relief used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
The marble portrait bust is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 7
Radiography of the oak chest identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 8
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the linen sampler following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 9
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the silk altar frontal following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Polarised light microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 12
The marble portrait bust is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.
