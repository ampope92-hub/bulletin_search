Collections Care Digest, issue 72

## Item 1
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 2
Radiography of the vellum choir book identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the plaster wall relief with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 4
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the pewter tankard identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
X-ray fluorescence of the bronze votive figure identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 6
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silver caddy spoon with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 9
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the marble portrait bust identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the vellum choir book with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 11
The linen sampler is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.
