Collections Care Digest, issue 73

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 2
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the lacquer writing box was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 3
Polarised light microscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a cellulose ether gel, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Infrared spectroscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
Radiography of the plaster wall relief identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
A through crack in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 11
The lacquer writing box was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 12
Radiography of the leather bookbinding identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.
