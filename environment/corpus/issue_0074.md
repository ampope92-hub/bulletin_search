Collections Care Digest, issue 74

## Item 1
The pewter tankard is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used an isinglass solution, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the plaster wall relief used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the marble portrait bust used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 7
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the glass roundel identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the ivory hair comb identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 12
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.
