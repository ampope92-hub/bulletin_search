Studio Bulletin, issue 80

## Item 1
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
The porcelain dish is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed insect frass from the papier-mache tea tray with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the wool tapestry with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 5
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the papier-mache tea tray used an isinglass solution, applied by nebuliser in several passes. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
Raman microscopy of the wool tapestry identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
The plaster wall relief was rehoused in an aluminium honeycomb panel, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 9
The pewter tankard was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the lacquer writing box used a cellulose ether gel, applied by nebuliser in several passes. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
