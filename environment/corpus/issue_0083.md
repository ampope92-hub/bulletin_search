Studio Bulletin, issue 83

## Item 1
A failed earlier repair in the papier-mache tea tray was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 2
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a wheat starch paste, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the lacquer writing box used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the ivory hair comb with grated vinyl crumb, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the ivory hair comb identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 6
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the papier-mache tea tray following consolidation of the powdering surface. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 7
An infestation of silverfish in the vellum choir book was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 8
A through crack in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with hidden hardwood pegs. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 9
X-ray fluorescence of the leather bookbinding identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 11
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.
