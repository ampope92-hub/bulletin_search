Collections Care Digest, issue 88

## Item 1
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
X-ray fluorescence of the leather bookbinding identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the plaster wall relief with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 6
Polarised light microscopy of the glass roundel identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 7
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the leather bookbinding identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a seaweed-derived size, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 9
The plaster wall relief is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
The lacquer writing box is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the ivory hair comb with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.
