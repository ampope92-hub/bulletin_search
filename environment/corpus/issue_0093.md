Conservation Notes, issue 93

## Item 1
The ivory hair comb is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 2
The lacquer writing box was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 3
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 4
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the marble portrait bust identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the porcelain dish identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 6
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 7
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used an isinglass solution, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 9
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a seaweed-derived size, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
The silk altar frontal is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
X-ray fluorescence of the wool tapestry identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
The silk altar frontal was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.
