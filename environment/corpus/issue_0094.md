Conservation Notes, issue 94

## Item 1
The porcelain dish is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 2
The glass roundel is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 3
Radiography of the plaster wall relief identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 4
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 5
A failed earlier repair in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 6
The silk altar frontal is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the ivory hair comb with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a seaweed-derived size, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the vellum choir book with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the leather bookbinding with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 12
The oak chest is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.
