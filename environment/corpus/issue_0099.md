Collections Care Digest, issue 99

## Item 1
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 2
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 3
Polarised light microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the plaster wall relief following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
The lacquer writing box is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a cellulose ether gel, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 8
The silk altar frontal was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with silk crepeline. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the vellum choir book with a buffered enzyme solution, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
