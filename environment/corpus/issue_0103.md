Studio Bulletin, issue 103

## Item 1
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 2
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 3
Infrared spectroscopy of the bronze votive figure identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
Polarised light microscopy of the linen sampler identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
Infrared spectroscopy of the pewter tankard identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 6
The linen sampler was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with polyester wadding. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the vellum choir book with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 9
X-ray fluorescence of the lacquer writing box identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.
