Studio Bulletin, issue 105

## Item 1
Radiography of the lacquer writing box identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the ivory hair comb with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the linen sampler with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used an isinglass solution, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the vellum choir book with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 7
X-ray fluorescence of the plaster wall relief identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the pewter tankard following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
The leather bookbinding is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
The ivory hair comb was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.
