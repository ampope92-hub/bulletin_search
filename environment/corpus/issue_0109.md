Studio Bulletin, issue 109

## Item 1
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 2
Raman microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a warm fish glue, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 5
Infrared spectroscopy of the leather bookbinding identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 6
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the wool tapestry following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 7
The bronze votive figure was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 8
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the marble portrait bust used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 9
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
The vellum choir book is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 11
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the lacquer writing box following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 12
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the papier-mache tea tray used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.
