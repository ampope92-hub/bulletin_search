Collections Care Digest, issue 111

## Item 1
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 2
The marble portrait bust is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 3
The oak chest is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 4
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the papier-mache tea tray used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 5
An infestation of silverfish in the vellum choir book was treated by localised heat treatment. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 6
The pewter tankard was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with washed cotton jersey. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 7
The porcelain dish is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 8
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with polyester wadding. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 10
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a wheat starch paste, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 11
Polarised light microscopy of the porcelain dish identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a microcrystalline wax, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
