Collections Care Digest, issue 117

## Item 1
The ivory hair comb was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with polyester wadding. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 2
Infrared spectroscopy of the papier-mache tea tray identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the wool tapestry with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the ivory hair comb with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
The leather bookbinding is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 6
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used an isinglass solution, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the ivory hair comb identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 8
