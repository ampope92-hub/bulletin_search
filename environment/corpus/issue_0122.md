Studio Bulletin, issue 122

## Item 1
The silk altar frontal is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 2
Polarised light microscopy of the pewter tankard identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
The vellum choir book is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 4
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the bronze votive figure identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
An old split along the grain in the oak chest was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 6
Radiography of the vellum choir book identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the glass roundel following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 8
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the glass roundel following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 9
The pewter tankard is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the lacquer writing box was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 11
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.
