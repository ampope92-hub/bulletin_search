Collections Care Digest, issue 123

## Item 1
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the ivory hair comb used a seaweed-derived size, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 2
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 3
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a seaweed-derived size, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
A failed earlier repair in the oak chest was rejoined with hidden hardwood pegs. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 6
Radiography of the vellum choir book identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 7
A shattered rim in the porcelain dish was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the linen sampler identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 10
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used a wheat starch paste, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the wool tapestry with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
X-ray fluorescence of the plaster wall relief identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.
