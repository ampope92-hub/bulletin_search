Conservation Notes, issue 124

## Item 1
The glass roundel is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 2
The linen sampler was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 3
Raman microscopy of the wool tapestry identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
The pewter tankard is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 6
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the linen sampler was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 8
The bronze votive figure is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the oak chest was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 10
A detached handle in the oak chest was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 11
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the silver caddy spoon with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
