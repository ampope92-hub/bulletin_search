Conservation Notes, issue 129

## Item 1
The glass roundel is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 2
A failed earlier repair in the silver caddy spoon was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 3
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the plaster wall relief identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
Infrared spectroscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
The pewter tankard is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the marble portrait bust with grated vinyl crumb, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 7
The oak chest is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the marble portrait bust with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 10
A detached handle in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 11
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a warm fish glue, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.
