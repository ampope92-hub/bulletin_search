Conservation Notes, issue 130

## Item 1
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a seaweed-derived size, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the plaster wall relief used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
A failed earlier repair in the vellum choir book was rejoined with thread-by-thread bridging. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 5
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 6
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the papier-mache tea tray with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 8
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the ivory hair comb used a seaweed-derived size, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the leather bookbinding with grated vinyl crumb, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the glass roundel with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Infrared spectroscopy of the papier-mache tea tray identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.
