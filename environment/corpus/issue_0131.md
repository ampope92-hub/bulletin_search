Conservation Notes, issue 131

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silk altar frontal with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 2
A detached handle in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with hidden hardwood pegs. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 3
The lacquer writing box is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 4
The linen sampler is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 5
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the linen sampler following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 6
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 8
A through crack in the silver caddy spoon was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 9
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 10
Radiography of the silver caddy spoon identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 11
A detached handle in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 12
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.
