Collections Care Digest, issue 136

## Item 1
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the bronze votive figure identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 2
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the glass roundel following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 3
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the linen sampler following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 4
A shattered rim in the silver caddy spoon was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the oak chest with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 6
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the wool tapestry was treated by localised heat treatment. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the vellum choir book with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 8
A detached handle in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with hidden hardwood pegs. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 9
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the silk altar frontal was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 12
The leather bookbinding is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.
