Conservation Notes, issue 137

## Item 1
The bronze votive figure is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 2
An old split along the grain in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silk altar frontal with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the plaster wall relief with a cellulose poultice, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the marble portrait bust following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed insect frass from the wool tapestry with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 7
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the plaster wall relief used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 8
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the oak chest was treated by localised heat treatment. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 9
X-ray fluorescence of the papier-mache tea tray identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
Infrared spectroscopy of the bronze votive figure identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 11
X-ray fluorescence of the oak chest identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the wool tapestry was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.
