Collections Care Digest, issue 141

## Item 1
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 4
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the oak chest following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 5
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the linen sampler identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 6
The pewter tankard was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 7
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the oak chest identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the marble portrait bust with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
The silk altar frontal is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 11
Raman microscopy of the linen sampler identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the glass roundel following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.
