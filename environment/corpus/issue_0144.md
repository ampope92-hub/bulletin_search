Collections Care Digest, issue 144

## Item 1
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the plaster wall relief with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 2
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a sunken window mat, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 3
X-ray fluorescence of the marble portrait bust identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the pewter tankard with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 5
A through crack in the papier-mache tea tray was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 6
The lacquer writing box is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 7
X-ray fluorescence of the marble portrait bust identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silk altar frontal with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 9
The ivory hair comb is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 10
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 11
Raman microscopy of the lacquer writing box identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 12
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.
