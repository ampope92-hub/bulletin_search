Conservation Notes, issue 150

## Item 1
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the marble portrait bust following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 2
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the leather bookbinding following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 3
A failed earlier repair in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 4
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 5
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the linen sampler was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 7
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the ivory hair comb following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the plaster wall relief identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 10
Polarised light microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silver caddy spoon with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 12
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.
