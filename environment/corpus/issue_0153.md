Studio Bulletin, issue 153

## Item 1
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the silk altar frontal with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a seaweed-derived size, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the glass roundel with a cellulose poultice, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 5
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the oak chest was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 6
Raman microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 7
The porcelain dish is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 8
The porcelain dish is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
Polarised light microscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 11
Radiography of the pewter tankard identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a warm fish glue, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
