Conservation Notes, issue 157

## Item 1
X-ray fluorescence of the lacquer writing box identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 2
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 3
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the pewter tankard following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 4
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the vellum choir book following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 5
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 6
Raman microscopy of the wool tapestry identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
A failed earlier repair in the leather bookbinding was rejoined with thread-by-thread bridging. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 8
The wool tapestry is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
The pewter tankard is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
A detached handle in the oak chest was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 12
The marble portrait bust is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.
