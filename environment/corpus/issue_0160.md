Conservation Notes, issue 160

## Item 1
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 2
The pewter tankard is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 3
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the plaster wall relief following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 4
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the bronze votive figure following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 6
The bronze votive figure is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 7
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a warm fish glue, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 8
The silk altar frontal was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with polyester wadding. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 9
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 11
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
The bronze votive figure was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.
