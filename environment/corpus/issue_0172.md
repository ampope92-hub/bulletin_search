Conservation Notes, issue 172

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the wool tapestry with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 4
Radiography of the marble portrait bust identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
The silk altar frontal was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 7
The porcelain dish is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the linen sampler identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the linen sampler identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
