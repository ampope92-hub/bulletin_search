Collections Care Digest, issue 175

## Item 1
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the lacquer writing box used a warm fish glue, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 2
An infestation of silverfish in the ivory hair comb was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 3
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with polyester wadding. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 4
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the oak chest with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a seaweed-derived size, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 10
The linen sampler is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
X-ray fluorescence of the vellum choir book identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
