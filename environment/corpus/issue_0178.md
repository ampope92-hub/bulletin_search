Conservation Notes, issue 178

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the vellum choir book with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed insect frass from the wool tapestry with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 4
Infrared spectroscopy of the porcelain dish identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the pewter tankard following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 6
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the lacquer writing box following consolidation of the powdering surface. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the bronze votive figure identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used a wheat starch paste, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
The leather bookbinding was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.
