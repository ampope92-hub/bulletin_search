Conservation Notes, issue 179

## Item 1
The vellum choir book is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 2
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the pewter tankard following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
The silver caddy spoon was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed insect frass from the silk altar frontal with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
The silk altar frontal is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the ivory hair comb with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the glass roundel with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the leather bookbinding identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 11
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 12
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the ivory hair comb following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.
