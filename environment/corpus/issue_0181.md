Collections Care Digest, issue 181

## Item 1
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the oak chest following removal of the earlier coating. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the silk altar frontal with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the silk altar frontal with a buffered enzyme solution, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the lacquer writing box following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 6
The porcelain dish is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the lacquer writing box identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 8
The glass roundel is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the papier-mache tea tray following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the wool tapestry with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
