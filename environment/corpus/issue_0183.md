Conservation Notes, issue 183

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
Infrared spectroscopy of the bronze votive figure identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
Raman microscopy of the glass roundel identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
The bronze votive figure is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 5
The vellum choir book is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 6
X-ray fluorescence of the bronze votive figure identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 8
Infrared spectroscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
X-ray fluorescence of the oak chest identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 10
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the papier-mache tea tray used a seaweed-derived size, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 11
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the oak chest was treated by localised heat treatment. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.
