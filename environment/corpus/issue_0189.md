Collections Care Digest, issue 189

## Item 1
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 2
The ivory hair comb is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the plaster wall relief with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 6
Treatment of the flaking paint on the plaster wall relief used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the oak chest identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
Radiography of the plaster wall relief identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the lacquer writing box following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used an isinglass solution, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 12
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.
