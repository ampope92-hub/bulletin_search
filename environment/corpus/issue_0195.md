Studio Bulletin, issue 195

## Item 1
A through crack in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 2
A failed earlier repair in the plaster wall relief was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 3
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 4
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the lacquer writing box following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 5
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 7
The leather bookbinding is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the lacquer writing box identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 10
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 12
Raman microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.
