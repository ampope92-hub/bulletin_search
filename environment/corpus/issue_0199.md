Studio Bulletin, issue 199

## Item 1
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the porcelain dish with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 4
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the leather bookbinding with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the ivory hair comb with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 7
The lacquer writing box was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 8
Polarised light microscopy of the linen sampler identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 9
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a drop-back clamshell box, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 10
The pewter tankard is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
The oak chest is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
The oak chest is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.
