Conservation Notes, issue 201

## Item 1
An infestation of silverfish in the papier-mache tea tray was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 4
An old split along the grain in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the wool tapestry with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Raman microscopy of the pewter tankard identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the oak chest identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 8
The oak chest is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 10
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the silver caddy spoon following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 11
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the papier-mache tea tray identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the pewter tankard identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.
