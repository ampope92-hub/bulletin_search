Collections Care Digest, issue 203

## Item 1
Polarised light microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 2
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the linen sampler was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 3
An infestation of silverfish in the ivory hair comb was treated by localised heat treatment. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 4
Polarised light microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 5
Radiography of the linen sampler identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 6
The glass roundel is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
Radiography of the oak chest identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the lacquer writing box was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 11
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the oak chest following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 12
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.
