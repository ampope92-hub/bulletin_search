Studio Bulletin, issue 205

## Item 1
Infrared spectroscopy of the plaster wall relief identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 2
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
A failed earlier repair in the porcelain dish was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 5
A shattered rim in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 7
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a wheat starch paste, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 9
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 11
The lacquer writing box is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
Infrared spectroscopy of the vellum choir book identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.
