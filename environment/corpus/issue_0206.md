Collections Care Digest, issue 206

## Item 1
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the bronze votive figure following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silver caddy spoon with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4

Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 5

## Item 6
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the oak chest following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 7
The glass roundel is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 8
X-ray fluorescence of the papier-mache tea tray identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 11
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a seaweed-derived size, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.
