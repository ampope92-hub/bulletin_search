Conservation Notes, issue 211

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 2
X-ray fluorescence of the leather bookbinding identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used an isinglass solution, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 6
Infrared spectroscopy of the pewter tankard identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 7
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the bronze votive figure following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 8
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the marble portrait bust with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the glass roundel with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
The vellum choir book is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 12
The plaster wall relief is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.
