Studio Bulletin, issue 213

## Item 1
The ivory hair comb is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 2
The silk altar frontal is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 3
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used a wheat starch paste, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 4
X-ray fluorescence of the linen sampler identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the silk altar frontal was treated by localised heat treatment. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silver caddy spoon with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the ivory hair comb with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed insect frass from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 11
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the marble portrait bust following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.
