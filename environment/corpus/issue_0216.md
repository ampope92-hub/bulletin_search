Collections Care Digest, issue 216

## Item 1
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
The silver caddy spoon was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 3
X-ray fluorescence of the marble portrait bust identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed insect frass from the vellum choir book with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 6
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the marble portrait bust used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 7
The glass roundel is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 8
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the pewter tankard identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 10
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the linen sampler following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
A detached handle in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.
