Studio Bulletin, issue 218

## Item 1
The glass roundel is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 2
Infrared spectroscopy of the oak chest identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 3
An infestation of silverfish in the papier-mache tea tray was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 4
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 7
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Polarised light microscopy of the leather bookbinding identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed insect frass from the leather bookbinding with grated vinyl crumb, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
X-ray fluorescence of the lacquer writing box identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the pewter tankard with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the leather bookbinding with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
