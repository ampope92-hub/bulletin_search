Collections Care Digest, issue 3

## Item 1
X-ray fluorescence of the ivory hair comb identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed insect frass from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 4
An infestation of silverfish in the leather bookbinding was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 5
A shattered rim in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 6
A through crack in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the marble portrait bust used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the silk altar frontal identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the leather bookbinding following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 10
Raman microscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 11
The wool tapestry is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 12
A through crack in the porcelain dish was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.
