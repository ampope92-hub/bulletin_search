Conservation Notes, issue 8

## Item 1
The glass roundel was rehoused in an aluminium honeycomb panel, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 2
A failed earlier repair in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 4
Raman microscopy of the vellum choir book identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a wheat starch paste, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 6
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the linen sampler was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 7
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the oak chest following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the silver caddy spoon with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 10
The lacquer writing box is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
An infestation of silverfish in the ivory hair comb was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 12
A detached handle in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.
