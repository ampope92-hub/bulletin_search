Conservation Notes, issue 20

## Item 1
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the papier-mache tea tray following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 2
Treatment of the flaking paint on the lacquer writing box used a warm fish glue, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 3
Raman microscopy of the glass roundel identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
The vellum choir book is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 5
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 6
Radiography of the bronze votive figure identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the leather bookbinding with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
Radiography of the oak chest identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.
