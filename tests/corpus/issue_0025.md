Studio Bulletin, issue 25

## Item 1
Raman microscopy of the pewter tankard identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 2
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 3
Radiography of the vellum choir book identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the silk altar frontal following consolidation of the powdering surface. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 8
The silk altar frontal is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 9
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 10
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the lacquer writing box was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 11
A through crack in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 12
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the lacquer writing box was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.
