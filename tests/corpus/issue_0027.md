Collections Care Digest, issue 27

## Item 1
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a microcrystalline wax, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 2
Raman microscopy of the leather bookbinding identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the silver caddy spoon following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed insect frass from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 7
Infrared spectroscopy of the vellum choir book identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 8
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the papier-mache tea tray following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
The marble portrait bust was rehoused in an aluminium honeycomb panel, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 11
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silk altar frontal with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
