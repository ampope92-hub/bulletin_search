Studio Bulletin, issue 29

## Item 1
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 3
X-ray fluorescence of the lacquer writing box identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silk altar frontal with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 5
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with silk crepeline. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 6
The glass roundel is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 7
An infestation of silverfish in the leather bookbinding was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
The bronze votive figure was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 10
The silk altar frontal is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
Infrared spectroscopy of the oak chest identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed insect frass from the vellum choir book with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
