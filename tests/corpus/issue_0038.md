Conservation Notes, issue 38

## Item 1
Infrared spectroscopy of the wool tapestry identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 3
Radiography of the silver caddy spoon identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 4
A failed earlier repair in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 5
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the bronze votive figure following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 6
The linen sampler is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
Infrared spectroscopy of the glass roundel identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the ivory hair comb with grated vinyl crumb, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the wool tapestry with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
