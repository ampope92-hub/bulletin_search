Collections Care Digest, issue 39

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 2
The plaster wall relief was rehoused in an aluminium honeycomb panel, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 3
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the wool tapestry identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
The porcelain dish is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the pewter tankard with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 9
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the lacquer writing box following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 10
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 11
The porcelain dish is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.
