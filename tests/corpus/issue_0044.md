Collections Care Digest, issue 44

## Item 1
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 2
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the oak chest was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 3
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used a seaweed-derived size, applied by nebuliser in several passes. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
The oak chest is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 5
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 6
Infrared spectroscopy of the lacquer writing box identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the marble portrait bust with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 8
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 9
A through crack in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silver caddy spoon with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 11
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.
