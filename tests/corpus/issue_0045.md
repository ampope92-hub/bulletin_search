Collections Care Digest, issue 45

## Item 1
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a sunken window mat, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the silver caddy spoon with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
The porcelain dish is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 5
A failed earlier repair in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 6
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 7
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a wheat starch paste, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 9
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the linen sampler was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 10
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the plaster wall relief used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
