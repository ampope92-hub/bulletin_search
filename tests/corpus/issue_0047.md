Conservation Notes, issue 47

## Item 1
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the vellum choir book following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 2
A failed earlier repair in the silver caddy spoon was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 3
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a sunken window mat, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 4
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silver caddy spoon with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the plaster wall relief used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 8
The silk altar frontal is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 9
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the papier-mache tea tray used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 10
Radiography of the bronze votive figure identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 11
Raman microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the plaster wall relief with a cellulose poultice, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.
