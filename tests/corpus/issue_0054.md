Studio Bulletin, issue 54

## Item 1
A shattered rim in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the vellum choir book with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
The linen sampler is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a warm fish glue, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the oak chest following removal of the earlier coating. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the oak chest identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
A failed earlier repair in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the vellum choir book with grated vinyl crumb, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 11
Raman microscopy of the papier-mache tea tray identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the pewter tankard with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
