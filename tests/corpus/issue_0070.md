Studio Bulletin, issue 70

## Item 1
The plaster wall relief is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the glass roundel with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a seaweed-derived size, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
X-ray fluorescence of the leather bookbinding identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 6
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the silk altar frontal following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 7
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the wool tapestry following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 8
The marble portrait bust is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a cellulose ether gel, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the marble portrait bust used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 12
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the lacquer writing box was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.
