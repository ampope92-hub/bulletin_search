Studio Bulletin, issue 78

## Item 1
Polarised light microscopy of the pewter tankard identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the vellum choir book identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 6
Infrared spectroscopy of the glass roundel identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the marble portrait bust used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the marble portrait bust used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 10
Infrared spectroscopy of the linen sampler identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 11
An infestation of silverfish in the vellum choir book was treated by localised heat treatment. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 12
Infrared spectroscopy of the oak chest identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.
