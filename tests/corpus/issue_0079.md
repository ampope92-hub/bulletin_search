Studio Bulletin, issue 79

## Item 1
Radiography of the glass roundel identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed insect frass from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 3
The oak chest was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 4
Radiography of the lacquer writing box identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the ivory hair comb following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 6
Polarised light microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
The oak chest is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 8
The porcelain dish is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 9
The lacquer writing box is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 10
The lacquer writing box is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 12
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.
