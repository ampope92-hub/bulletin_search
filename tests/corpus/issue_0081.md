Collections Care Digest, issue 81

## Item 1
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the silver caddy spoon following consolidation of the powdering surface. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 2
Radiography of the silver caddy spoon identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used an isinglass solution, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the linen sampler was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 5
The lacquer writing box was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the vellum choir book with a buffered enzyme solution, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 7
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the lacquer writing box identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
Polarised light microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 11
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 12
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.
