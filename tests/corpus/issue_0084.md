Collections Care Digest, issue 84

## Item 1
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the silk altar frontal following consolidation of the powdering surface. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 2
The marble portrait bust is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 3
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 4
A shattered rim in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 5
The oak chest is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
A failed earlier repair in the vellum choir book was rejoined with thread-by-thread bridging. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 8
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 9
The leather bookbinding is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the silk altar frontal with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Polarised light microscopy of the oak chest identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 12
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.
