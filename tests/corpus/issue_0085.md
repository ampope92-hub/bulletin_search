Conservation Notes, issue 85

## Item 1
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a wheat starch paste, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
The silk altar frontal is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 4
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 5
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the plaster wall relief following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 6
The plaster wall relief was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 7
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the oak chest following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 8
The marble portrait bust is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 9
Infrared spectroscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the ivory hair comb with grated vinyl crumb, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the marble portrait bust with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.
