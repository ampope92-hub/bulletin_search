Conservation Notes, issue 89

## Item 1
The linen sampler was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 2
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a sunken window mat, padded with unbuffered tissue. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 3
The leather bookbinding is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 4
The oak chest is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 6
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the marble portrait bust used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a seaweed-derived size, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used an isinglass solution, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 9
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
A through crack in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 11
A through crack in the papier-mache tea tray was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 12
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the linen sampler was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.
