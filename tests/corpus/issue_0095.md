Conservation Notes, issue 95

## Item 1
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used an isinglass solution, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 3
Raman microscopy of the bronze votive figure identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 5
The silver caddy spoon is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the wool tapestry used a seaweed-derived size, applied under a heated spatula. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 7
The glass roundel is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the wool tapestry identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the marble portrait bust with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the bronze votive figure identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 11
An infestation of silverfish in the papier-mache tea tray was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the lacquer writing box used a warm fish glue, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
