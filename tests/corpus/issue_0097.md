Studio Bulletin, issue 97

## Item 1
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 2
The linen sampler is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 3
A detached handle in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 4
Radiography of the wool tapestry identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the marble portrait bust used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 7
The marble portrait bust is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the linen sampler identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
Polarised light microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
The silver caddy spoon was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 11
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the oak chest following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the pewter tankard with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
