Collections Care Digest, issue 100

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the ivory hair comb used a wheat starch paste, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 2
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the silver caddy spoon identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the wool tapestry with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
X-ray fluorescence of the glass roundel identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
Infrared spectroscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the silver caddy spoon with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the marble portrait bust with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 9
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a wheat starch paste, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 10
The leather bookbinding is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the silk altar frontal following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 12
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the lacquer writing box used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
