Collections Care Digest, issue 102

## Item 1
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a seaweed-derived size, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the vellum choir book with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the bronze votive figure following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 4
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with polyester wadding. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 5
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 6
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the leather bookbinding following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 8
Infrared spectroscopy of the ivory hair comb identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 9
The porcelain dish was rehoused in an aluminium honeycomb panel, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
The leather bookbinding is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.
