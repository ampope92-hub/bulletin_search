Studio Bulletin, issue 104

## Item 1
The oak chest is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with polyester wadding. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 4
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the oak chest following consolidation of the powdering surface. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 7
The porcelain dish is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 8
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the vellum choir book with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the papier-mache tea tray used a microcrystalline wax, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed insect frass from the vellum choir book with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 12
The pewter tankard is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.
