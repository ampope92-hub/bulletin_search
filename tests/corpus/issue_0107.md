Studio Bulletin, issue 107

## Item 1
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a wheat starch paste, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the silver caddy spoon with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silk altar frontal with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 6
X-ray fluorescence of the ivory hair comb identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with a buffered enzyme solution, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 8
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the oak chest identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a seaweed-derived size, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 12
The porcelain dish is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.
