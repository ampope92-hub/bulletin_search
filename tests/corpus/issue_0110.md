Collections Care Digest, issue 110

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the plaster wall relief used a microcrystalline wax, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 3
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
Infrared spectroscopy of the leather bookbinding identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 5
A detached handle in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 6
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
The bronze votive figure is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 8
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 9
A through crack in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with hidden hardwood pegs. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 10
The ivory hair comb is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the pewter tankard with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
