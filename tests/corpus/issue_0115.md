Collections Care Digest, issue 115

## Item 1
X-ray fluorescence of the linen sampler identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 2
Infrared spectroscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
Radiography of the wool tapestry identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
Polarised light microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
Radiography of the wool tapestry identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the silver caddy spoon with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 7
X-ray fluorescence of the glass roundel identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the porcelain dish identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 9
A detached handle in the glass roundel was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 10
The linen sampler was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with silk crepeline. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 11
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the wool tapestry was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.
