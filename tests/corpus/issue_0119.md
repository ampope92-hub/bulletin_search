Collections Care Digest, issue 119

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 3
A failed earlier repair in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a shaped balsa fill. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 4
The glass roundel is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 5
The marble portrait bust is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 6
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 7
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 8
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the silver caddy spoon following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
The bronze votive figure was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 11
The marble portrait bust is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed insect frass from the oak chest with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.
