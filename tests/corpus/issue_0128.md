Collections Care Digest, issue 128

## Item 1
Radiography of the leather bookbinding identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the leather bookbinding following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 3
The wool tapestry is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 4
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used a wheat starch paste, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the vellum choir book with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Treatment of the cupped and tented ground on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 7
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the plaster wall relief identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
Raman microscopy of the bronze votive figure identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 10
Polarised light microscopy of the linen sampler identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a seaweed-derived size, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.
