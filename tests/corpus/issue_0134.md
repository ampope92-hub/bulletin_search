Collections Care Digest, issue 134

## Item 1
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the oak chest following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 2
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 3
Raman microscopy of the bronze votive figure identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
The bronze votive figure is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 5
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 6
The silk altar frontal is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 7
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
The porcelain dish is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the porcelain dish used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
