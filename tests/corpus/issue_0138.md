Conservation Notes, issue 138

## Item 1
The plaster wall relief is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the papier-mache tea tray used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 4
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a wheat starch paste, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the vellum choir book identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 8
Infrared spectroscopy of the glass roundel identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 9
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the oak chest was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the leather bookbinding with a buffered enzyme solution, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the silk altar frontal used a warm fish glue, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.
