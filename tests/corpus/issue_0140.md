Studio Bulletin, issue 140

## Item 1
Polarised light microscopy of the silk altar frontal identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the wool tapestry with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the leather bookbinding following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working from the least sensitive area outward. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the leather bookbinding with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 7
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the linen sampler following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 8
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the silk altar frontal was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 9
Radiography of the oak chest identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the marble portrait bust used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a cellulose ether gel, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.
