Conservation Notes, issue 151

## Item 1
The pewter tankard is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 2
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the linen sampler was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 3
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 4
An infestation of silverfish in the ivory hair comb was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 5
A shattered rim in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 6
An infestation of powderpost beetle in the oak chest was treated by controlled freezing with staged warming. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 7
A through crack in the lacquer writing box was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 8
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the vellum choir book used an isinglass solution, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
Raman microscopy of the wool tapestry identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 11
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the bronze votive figure following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
