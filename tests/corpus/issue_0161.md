Collections Care Digest, issue 161

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the marble portrait bust used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 2
A detached handle in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a pin of matching alloy. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 3
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the papier-mache tea tray identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 6
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 7
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the silk altar frontal with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the ivory hair comb with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the lacquer writing box with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 11
A brushed dammar varnish was applied to the ivory hair comb following consolidation of the powdering surface. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the plaster wall relief with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
