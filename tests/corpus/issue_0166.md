Studio Bulletin, issue 166

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the glass roundel with a cellulose poultice, working in short overlapping passes. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 2
Radiography of the ivory hair comb identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the ivory hair comb used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 4
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a microcrystalline wax, applied by nebuliser in several passes. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 5
A shattered rim in the porcelain dish was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced.

## Item 6
X-ray fluorescence of the silver caddy spoon identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 7
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the pewter tankard following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 8
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
Radiography of the papier-mache tea tray identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
The glass roundel is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 11
Infrared spectroscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the leather bookbinding with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
