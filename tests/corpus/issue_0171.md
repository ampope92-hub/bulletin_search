Studio Bulletin, issue 171

## Item 1
Infrared spectroscopy of the bronze votive figure identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
The oak chest is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 3
The vellum choir book is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 4
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
A through crack in the bronze votive figure was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light.

## Item 7
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the lacquer writing box used a cellulose ether gel, applied by nebuliser in several passes. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 8
Radiography of the papier-mache tea tray identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 9
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the lacquer writing box following consolidation of the powdering surface. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the marble portrait bust with a cellulose poultice, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 11
The vellum choir book was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 12
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the marble portrait bust following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.
