Collections Care Digest, issue 180

## Item 1
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the oak chest used a microcrystalline wax, applied through fine tissue. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Infrared spectroscopy of the glass roundel identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed adhesive residue from old tape from the porcelain dish with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 7
