Conservation Notes, issue 182

## Item 1
Treatment of the flaking paint on the plaster wall relief used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 2
The silk altar frontal was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 3
An infestation of carpet beetle larvae in the wool tapestry was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Trap counts have stayed at zero since.

## Item 4
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the ivory hair comb used a seaweed-derived size, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 5
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a seaweed-derived size, applied under a heated spatula. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 6
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the lacquer writing box used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 7
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 8
The lacquer writing box is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
The lacquer writing box is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the papier-mache tea tray with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 12
The leather bookbinding is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.
