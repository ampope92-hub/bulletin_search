Studio Bulletin, issue 184

## Item 1
Polarised light microscopy of the bronze votive figure identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 2
The ivory hair comb was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 3
Polarised light microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with polyester wadding. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 5
Radiography of the marble portrait bust identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 6
A failed earlier repair in the oak chest was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the oak chest identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 8
The plaster wall relief is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 9
An infestation of silverfish in the papier-mache tea tray was treated by isolation and monitoring with blunder traps. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Neighbouring storage was surveyed at the same time.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 11
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the plaster wall relief identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 12
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.
