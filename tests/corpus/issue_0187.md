Collections Care Digest, issue 187

## Item 1
The plaster wall relief is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 2
Treatment of the flaking paint on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the ivory hair comb with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the plaster wall relief used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 5
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the lacquer writing box used a dilute acrylic resin, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 6
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
X-ray fluorescence of the lacquer writing box identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 8
The bronze votive figure was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 9
The pewter tankard is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 10
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the lacquer writing box used a cellulose ether gel, applied by capillary feed along the edge. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 11
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the leather bookbinding identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.
