Collections Care Digest, issue 188

## Item 1
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the wool tapestry with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 2
Radiography of the porcelain dish identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 3
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the papier-mache tea tray used an isinglass solution, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.

## Item 4
The bronze votive figure is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 5
The linen sampler was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 6
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the vellum choir book following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 7
Radiography of the linen sampler identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 8
Raman microscopy of the glass roundel identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
Infrared spectroscopy of the wool tapestry identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 10
X-ray fluorescence of the vellum choir book identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 12
The porcelain dish is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.
