Studio Bulletin, issue 194

## Item 1
Treatment of the crizzled surface on the glass roundel used a microcrystalline wax, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the linen sampler with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 3
Treatment of the flaking paint on the silver caddy spoon used a dilute acrylic resin, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the bronze votive figure following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 6
Polarised light microscopy of the pewter tankard identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
The vellum choir book is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 8
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the linen sampler identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
The bronze votive figure is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 10
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the ivory hair comb used a seaweed-derived size, applied under a heated spatula. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 11
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the linen sampler used a warm fish glue, applied through fine tissue. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.
