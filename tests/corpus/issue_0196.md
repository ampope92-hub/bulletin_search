Collections Care Digest, issue 196

## Item 1
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working with frequent renewal of the swab. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 2
An infestation of webbing clothes moth in the oak chest was treated by a fortnight in a low-oxygen atmosphere. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began.

## Item 3
The lacquer writing box is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 4
Treatment of the blistered varnish on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material.

## Item 5
The wool tapestry is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 6
Radiography of the marble portrait bust identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 7
The leather bookbinding is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed a discoloured natural resin coating from the linen sampler with a rigid chelating gel, working from the least sensitive area outward. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 10
Treatment of the flaking paint on the lacquer writing box used a cellulose ether gel, applied under gentle vacuum suction. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 11
Radiography of the vellum choir book identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the pewter tankard used a microcrystalline wax, applied by capillary feed along the edge. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material.
