Conservation Notes, issue 198

## Item 1
A buffed microcrystalline wax was applied to the silver caddy spoon following corrosion inhibition with an azole. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 2
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the leather bookbinding following consolidation of the powdering surface. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 3
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the papier-mache tea tray identified bast fibre in the warp and protein fibre in the weft. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 4
The leather bookbinding is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under filtered daylight only. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 5
Treatment of the delaminating veneer on the oak chest used a cellulose ether gel, applied under a heated spatula. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 6
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the marble portrait bust following toning of the fills with dry pigment. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 7
The ivory hair comb is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 8
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the ivory hair comb with grated vinyl crumb, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 9
The pewter tankard was rehoused in a barrier-film enclosure with an oxygen scavenger, padded with unbuffered tissue. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 10
Raman microscopy of the linen sampler identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 11
Raman microscopy of the porcelain dish identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 12
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the oak chest with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.
