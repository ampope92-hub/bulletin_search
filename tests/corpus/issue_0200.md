Studio Bulletin, issue 200

## Item 1
The glass roundel is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 2
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the vellum choir book with grated vinyl crumb, working in short overlapping passes. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 3
Treatment of the powdering pigment on the marble portrait bust used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied through fine tissue. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 4
The bronze votive figure is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 5
A through crack in the plaster wall relief was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 6
Polarised light microscopy of the silver caddy spoon identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the glass roundel identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 8
Radiography of the marble portrait bust identified an arsenic-bearing green in the border. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 9
The plaster wall relief was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed.

## Item 10
A matte cellulose ether film was applied to the lacquer writing box following removal of the earlier coating. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 11
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the wool tapestry with a buffered enzyme solution, working with frequent renewal of the swab. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 12
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a cellulose ether gel, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.
