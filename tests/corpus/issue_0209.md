Collections Care Digest, issue 209

## Item 1
The papier-mache tea tray was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with polyester wadding. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 2
A through crack in the plaster wall relief was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. The join is visible on close inspection by design. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the bronze votive figure with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working from the least sensitive area outward. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 4
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the vellum choir book identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. The finding is consistent with the attributed date. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 5
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the pewter tankard with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed insect frass from the lacquer writing box with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 7
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the oak chest used a warm fish glue, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal.

## Item 8

Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.
