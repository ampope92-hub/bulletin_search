Studio Bulletin, issue 214

## Item 1
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the pewter tankard with a pulsed laser at low fluence, working in one direction only. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 2
A failed earlier repair in the marble portrait bust was rejoined with a reversible animal glue. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. The repair can be taken apart again if required. The join is visible on close inspection by design.

## Item 3
The marble portrait bust was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with unbuffered tissue. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The housing carries a printed handling instruction.

## Item 4
Polarised light microscopy of the wool tapestry identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 5
The leather bookbinding is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. The specification was agreed with the curator. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 6
The vellum choir book is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes.

## Item 7
Raman microscopy of the oak chest identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 8
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the bronze votive figure following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 9
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at thirty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 10
The wool tapestry was rehoused in a coated brass tension strap, padded with polyester wadding. The housing carries a printed handling instruction. Handling is now possible without touching the surface. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted.

## Item 11
Polarised light microscopy of the marble portrait bust identified a mercury-based red beneath a later overpaint. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 12
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a dilute acrylic resin, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.
