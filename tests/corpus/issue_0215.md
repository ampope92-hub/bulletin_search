Conservation Notes, issue 215

## Item 1
Polarised light microscopy of the glass roundel identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 2
The papier-mache tea tray is displayed at forty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 3
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the wool tapestry with a buffered enzyme solution, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 4
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the vellum choir book following toning of the fills with dry pigment. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure. The finish was left to cure for a week before display.

## Item 5
The linen sampler is displayed at sixty per cent relative humidity under a hundred and fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck.

## Item 6
Cleaning removed sooty deposits from the ivory hair comb with a rigid chelating gel, working in short overlapping passes. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first.

## Item 7
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the plaster wall relief following removal of the earlier coating. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 8
The vellum choir book is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a fifty lux limit. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter. The specification was agreed with the curator.

## Item 9
Treatment of the brittle fibres on the leather bookbinding used a seaweed-derived size, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Testing preceded each change of material. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 10
The glass roundel is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. The specification was agreed with the curator. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 11
Treatment of the flaking paint on the bronze votive figure used a microcrystalline wax, applied with a fine brush under magnification. Testing preceded each change of material. Work stopped once further gain would have been marginal. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down.

## Item 12
Treatment of the cracked gilding on the marble portrait bust used a lime nanoparticle dispersion, applied under gentle vacuum suction. Photographs record the surface before and after setting down. Testing preceded each change of material. The treated area was left under light weight overnight.
