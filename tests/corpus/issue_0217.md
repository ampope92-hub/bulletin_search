Studio Bulletin, issue 217

## Item 1
Electron microscopy of a cross-section of the vellum choir book identified a double ground of chalk under lead white. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. The finding is consistent with the attributed date.

## Item 2
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the silver caddy spoon following removal of the earlier coating. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Application took place in a filtered air enclosure.

## Item 3
X-ray fluorescence of the pewter tankard identified a drying oil bound with a little pine resin. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed.

## Item 4
Cleaning removed greasy handling grime from the marble portrait bust with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in short overlapping passes. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. Clearance was checked under magnification at every stage.

## Item 5
Radiography of the silk altar frontal identified a reworked limb position in the underdrawing. Results were checked against laboratory reference material. Sampling was held to the minimum needed. Sample sites are marked on the accompanying diagram.

## Item 6
The marble portrait bust was rehoused in a carved inert foam cradle, padded with washed cotton jersey. The mount maker was consulted before the design was fixed. The fit was adjusted twice before it was accepted. Handling is now possible without touching the surface.

## Item 7
A sprayed low-molecular-weight resin was applied to the linen sampler following consolidation of the powdering surface. The finish was left to cure for a week before display. Two thin coats were preferred to one heavy one. Gloss was matched to an adjacent untreated area.

## Item 8
The leather bookbinding is displayed at fifty-five per cent relative humidity under a two hundred lux limit with annual rotation. Conditions are reviewed whenever the gallery layout changes. A buffer tray sits beneath the display deck. Loggers in the case are downloaded each quarter.

## Item 9
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the silk altar frontal with swabs of a hydrocarbon and alcohol blend, working in one direction only. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water. A small window was left untouched for future reference.

## Item 10
Cleaning removed hard calcareous crusts from the porcelain dish with a rigid chelating gel, working in one direction only. A small window was left untouched for future reference. Solubility was tested at three separate points first. Residues were rinsed away with deionised water.

## Item 11
An infestation of silverfish in the vellum choir book was treated by localised heat treatment. Debris was vacuumed away before treatment began. Trap counts have stayed at zero since. No live activity was seen at the follow-up inspection.

## Item 12
A failed earlier repair in the silk altar frontal was rejoined with thread-by-thread bridging. Alignment was dry-fitted before any adhesive was introduced. Clamping pressure was kept deliberately light. The repair can be taken apart again if required.
